I turn regulation into systems people actually use.

Juan Bracho, operations and compliance coordinator, on policy that survives contact with a 35-person company.

Most compliance work fails quietly. A policy gets written, filed, and routed around by everyone it was meant to govern. My job is the opposite: take the requirement — NIST, ISO, whatever the auditor brought this quarter — and turn it into something a real team will follow without being chased.

I came to it sideways. A five-year law degree in Venezuela, a master's in business law in Argentina, a data analytics bootcamp in Texas. The through-line is that I read the regulation and build the tool. When the spreadsheet stops scaling, I write the app.

Currently at HydroGraph Clean Power, a public company, where I wrote the governance suite, ran point on the external cybersecurity audit, and administer the systems the policies describe.
